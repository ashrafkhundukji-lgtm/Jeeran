# Jeeran Network — Explainer Video Script & Storyboard

**Format:** ~35–40 second full cut (feed/website), with a 15s TikTok/Reels trim marked below.
**Style direction:** Flat, clean 2D motion graphics matching the brand system (navy #042C53, coral #D85A30/#F0997B, warm off-white background, dot-grid + map-pin motif, rounded geometric shapes — no photography, no 3D). Think "modern fintech explainer," not "cartoon."
**Voiceover tone:** Warm, plain-spoken, a little conversational — not corporate-narrator, not hype-influencer. Confident and simple.
**Music direction:** Light, upbeat, minimal percussion — something that could sit under a product demo, not epic/cinematic.

---

## Full cut (~38s)

| # | Time | Visual | Voiceover / On-screen text | Notes |
|---|------|--------|------------------------------|-------|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | Jeeran logo mark draws itself on screen (the ج/J monogram traces in), dot-grid background fades in behind it | *(no VO — just a soft whoosh/chime sound)* | Hook must land in the first 2 seconds — logo animation should be fast and satisfying, not a slow reveal |
| 2 | 0:03–0:08 | A single shop icon appears on a stylized street/map. A QR stand icon pops onto its counter. | VO: "Every shop has customers walking by every day." | Keep the "shop" generic/iconic, not a specific illustrated business — this needs to feel universal |
| 3 | 0:08–0:14 | A phone icon approaches the QR stand, scans it (quick scan-line animation), then a wallet-pass card flies from the stand into the phone screen | VO: "One scan adds a free pass to their Wallet. No app, no signup." | This is the single most important beat — slow down slightly here relative to the rest, it's the "aha" moment |
| 4 | 0:14–0:22 | Zoom out to a map view. Multiple shop icons light up one by one as small offer cards float from each toward the phone icon (which now has a small pin marker showing its location) | VO: "From then on, they get real offers from shops nearby — automatically, on their lock screen." | This is where the "network" concept visually clicks — use the dot-grid/pin motif from the brand system here directly |
| 5 | 0:22–0:28 | Cut to the phone screen close-up: an offer notification appears, customer walks into a second shop icon, taps to show the barcode, shop icon "receives" it with a small credit/coin icon appearing above the ORIGINAL first shop icon (not the one they're standing in) | VO: "Every time they redeem anywhere, the shop that got them started keeps earning." | This is the hardest concept to convey visually — the arrow/credit needs to clearly travel back to shop #1, not shop #2, or the whole economic model won't land |
| 6 | 0:28–0:34 | Simple dashboard mockup slides up: a small bar chart + a number ticking up ("+12 customers this week") | VO: "Track exactly what it's earning you — in real time." | Reuse the dark dashboard-card visual style from the landing page concept for consistency |
| 7 | 0:34–0:38 | Logo + tagline centered, CTA text appears | VO: "Jeeran. Your neighbors are the best ones to advertise for you." On-screen text: "Get your free QR stand →" | End card should hold for at least 2 full seconds — don't cut away before the CTA is readable |

## 15s trim (TikTok/Reels — customer-facing cut)

Use beats 1, 3, 4, and 7 only. Drop the credit-flow/dashboard explanation entirely — that's a shop-owner concept, too complex for a 15s consumer-facing cut. Rewrite VO for this version:

> "No app. No signup. Just scan — and get real deals from shops near you. That's Jeeran."

This version should end on beat 4's "map lighting up" visual, not the dashboard — it's more visually satisfying and doesn't need the economic explanation a shop-owner cut requires.

## Deliverable variants to request from your editor/animator

1. **Full 38s cut** — website, Instagram/Facebook feed, YouTube pre-roll
2. **15s vertical cut** — TikTok, Reels, Stories (customer-facing only, per trim above)
3. **6–8s loop, no VO, captions only** — for silent autoplay contexts (Instagram feed default, in-shop display screens if you ever add those) — use beats 1, 3, and 7 only, compressed

## Asset handoff notes

- Brand colors, logo mark, and background texture are already defined in the landing page concepts — hand those over alongside this script so the animator matches them exactly rather than reinterpreting the brand.
- If working with an AI video tool (Runway, Pika) rather than a human animator, this script is written to be storyboard-literal enough to prompt scene-by-scene — treat each row as one generation.
