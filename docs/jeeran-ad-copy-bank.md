## AUDIENCE 1: Shop Owners (recruit them to host a QR stand)

### Variant A — Earnings-led
**Headline:** Your counter could be earning you customers right now.
**Body:** Host a free Jeeran QR stand and earn ad credit every time a nearby shop's offer gets scanned through it — then spend those credits to get your own offers in front of customers nearby. No agency, no ad budget, just your neighborhood.
**CTA:** Get your free QR stand

### Variant B — Simplicity-led
**Headline:** Zero-cost customer acquisition. One QR code.
**Body:** No app to build, no ad platform to learn. Print one QR code, put it on your counter, and start earning credit for every customer it brings into the network — even the ones who spend at other shops.
**CTA:** Join Jeeran free

### Variant C — Social proof / momentum (use once real numbers exist)
**Headline:** [X] shops are already earning from foot traffic they used to give away for free.
**Body:** Every customer who scans your QR stand becomes a source of ongoing ad credit — see exactly how many redemptions your stand has driven, live, in your dashboard.
**CTA:** See your shop's potential

**Platform notes:** Long-form body works for Instagram/Facebook feed captions. For WhatsApp Business outreach, trim to 2 lines max + CTA — this audience responds better to a direct 1:1 message than a broadcast ad.

## AUDIENCE 2: Customers (get them scanning)

### Variant A — Curiosity-led
**Headline:** Your neighborhood is full of deals you don't know about yet.
**Body:** Scan once, save it to your Wallet, and start getting real offers from shops near you — automatically. No app, no spam, just deals worth walking to.
**CTA:** Find a Jeeran stand near you

### Variant B — Friction-removal-led (best for TikTok/Reels — leads with "no app")
**Headline:** No app. No signup. Just deals.
**Body:** One scan adds a pass to your phone's Wallet. That's it — nearby shops' offers show up on your lock screen from then on.
**CTA:** Scan. Save. Save money.

### Variant C — Direct/transactional (best for in-shop signage near the QR stand itself)
**Headline:** Scan for a deal at the shop next door.
**Body:** Free, instant, no app required.
**CTA:** Scan me →

**Platform notes:** Variant B's punchy 3-line structure is built for 15–30s vertical video captions (TikTok/Reels). Variant C is meant for print/physical signage, not social — keep it that short on the actual QR stand card.
